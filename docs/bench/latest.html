Replica-local customer reads · lag is allowed. Each read uses one coherent locally applied committed replica snapshot without a per-read quorum fence. Startup recovery can first require quorum. State, code, and aliases may lag without a bound; requests across replicas can go backward in revision. This policy must not be confused with fresh-read performance. Mutations and the final per-group audit retain their fresh correctness requirements.

What the total means

Successful primary customer logical calls across all groups divided by the union of their synchronized load intervals, including in-flight tails. Excludes retries, explicit replays, workers, errors, warmup and drain. Percentiles merge bucket counts; they are not averages of group percentiles.

Static tenant-to-group assignment. Each tenant's stores, queues and rankings stay within one independent Raft group. No cross-group transaction or global atomic snapshot in this workload.

Hot-set weight is mixed with uniform store selection; it is not the final observed hot-store probability. Tenant selection is spread independently. Each tenant's stores and derived ranking remain in its assigned group. A busy tenant still shares one ordered writer lane; adding unrelated groups does not speed up that tenant's serial mutations.

Customer loops are closed-loop: each waits before issuing its next call. After the order cap, order choices become reads. Compare runs with the same workload, consistency, host, binary, and runtime settings. These are simultaneous independent-group measurements, not a globally atomic transaction or snapshot benchmark.
